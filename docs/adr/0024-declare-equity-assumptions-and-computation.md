# Declare Equity Assumptions and Computation

The optional Poker Math Overlay will derive pot odds, effective stacks, and SPR from observable facts, while equity uses explicitly selected opponent ranges with uniform legal holdings as the default and accounts for ties and relevant pot eligibility. It estimates the number of complete worlds required and enumerates all of them when that count is below a configured limit; otherwise it uses seeded Monte Carlo with an explicit sample count and reported uncertainty. Results retain their range assumptions and computation metadata, outs describe precisely what improvement is counted, and post-hand hidden-information reveal never changes normal decision-time analysis inputs.

A Complete Equity World assigns a legal holding to every live opponent plus one remaining community-card combination, with mutually compatible cards and weights determined by the declared range and chance model. The calculation evaluates showdown equity without future betting; unknown burn cards and folded players' hidden cards are marginalized rather than read from the authoritative deal. An enumerated result is exact under its declared assumptions, and ranges admitting no legal world produce an error rather than zero equity.
