# Visualize Policy Across Hypothetical Holdings

The Range Visualizer will evaluate a selected Policy across all legal hypothetical hole-card combinations in one fixed observable decision situation. Each holding receives an internally consistent derived input that replaces the Participant's current-Hand hole-card identities everywhere they appear in observable state and history, while retaining actions, public cards, stacks, pots, participant facts, and previous Hands. These immutable inputs are derived from the real snapshot without modifying CashGame or TableSessionRecord or creating another game instance.

`packages/research` owns legal hypothetical-holding enumeration, consistent derived ObservableGameViews, coordination of Policy evaluations through JobRunner-managed execution, and aggregation of validated results into exact-combination and hand-class analysis. Each Policy only evaluates the supplied view, and the Strategies framework owns validation/canonicalization of its output. CashGame does not generate hypothetical holdings; the frontend owns the grid, filters, highlighting, and interaction rather than duplicating the analysis computation. The same participant-safe workflow may analyze a live or historical decision without privileged hidden-state inspection.

It displays each holding's normalized Choice distribution and concrete Bet or Raise sizes through a 13-by-13 hand-class grid with exact combinations available, and supports action-type and exact-sizing filters. Each cell averages its legal combinations' distributions by action and exact sizing; an absent choice in a successful result contributes probability zero, while pending or failed evaluations remain explicitly unavailable and a cell without legal combinations is blocked. Highlighting the actual holding does not restrict evaluation, and the display aggregation does not infer holding likelihoods, reach probabilities, or range narrowing from preceding actions.

Every hypothetical holding uses the original decision's derived Policy evaluation seed, with the hypothetical holding and frozen input fingerprint distinguishing request and cache identities. Given the same implementation and configuration, evaluating the actual holding reproduces its normal analysis distribution. The visualizer displays Policy distributions without sampling an ActionSelector for each holding.
