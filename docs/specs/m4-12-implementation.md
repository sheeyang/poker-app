# M4-12 implementation evidence

The Table Owner fills and tunes a table without waiting for a pause. At either table mode they can add a Bot at any time and manage its Playing Strategy, stack, sitting out and Removal. At an Online Table they can change the Action Time Limit length, Allow Live Analysis and the seat-change permission between Hands. The owner still never acts for, moves, sits out, stands up, or changes the chips of another human, and sees neither their hole cards nor their analysis.

**Seat-change permission (engine).** `CashGame.setSeatChanges(allowed)` is a new between-Hands command (`set-seat-changes`, event `seat-changes-changed`). The permission lives in the table state, starting from the configuration's value, so `getView().configuration.allowSeatChanges` always shows the current value and `moveParticipant` stays the one place that enforces it. All other Game Configuration stays fixed. Replay, forking and restoration carry it, a historical decision snapshot shows the value as it stood then, and a version 1 record cannot contain the command. Rejections: `active-hand` (a completed Hand counts as between Hands), `invalid-seat-changes`, `seat-changes-unchanged`.

**Table options.** `POST /:id/set-table-options` (owner only, Online only, between Hands: the payout phase counts as part of the Hand) takes a non-empty partial `options` of `actionTimeLimitSeconds`, `allowLiveAnalysis` and `allowSeatChanges`. One request is one session revision, plus one poker revision when the seat-change permission changed. Everyone sees the new options in their next view and live update. An option set to its current value is acknowledged without a commit. Changing an option during a running Next-Hand Countdown leaves the countdown alone, and the Hand it deals uses the new limit: the owner cannot stall dealing by changing options, and nothing about eligibility depends on them. #43 owns the effects of `allowLiveAnalysis`.

**Bots.** `POST /:id/add-bot` binds a new Bot and seats it in one commit, at the named or lowest open Seat, with a buy-in within the limits, even during a Hand: Hand participants are frozen, so it is dealt in from the next Hand without a missed blind. Names are unique ignoring case among every Participant, departed ones included. `POST /:id/remove-bot` stands the Bot up, cashing it out, and leaves it off the table for good; a seated Bot in a Hand goes after it, as an After-Hand Request (`remove`) shown to everyone, and an unseated Bot goes at once. The owner's Bot sit-out during a Hand is deferred the same way; top-ups, Stand Up and resuming still wait for the Hand. Strategy changes work at any time.

**Who has left.** Every complete view lists `departedParticipantIds` (humans who left or were removed, removed Bots) once anyone has left, so the Trainer no longer offers Removal of a Guest who has gone, and lists neither departed Participants nor removed Bots in table options.

Server tests are in `apps/server/test/owner-controls.test.ts`: option changes (visible to everyone and live, restart, rejections, idempotency, no-op, Practice, during a Hand and payout, a running countdown), adding Bots at Practice and Online Tables mid-Hand and their first Hand, every add-Bot rejection, Bot management (strategy, top-up, sit out and resume, removal, a removed Bot's name), Bots in a Hand sat out or removed after it, the owner's attempts on another human (every kind refused, hole cards and analysis unseen), `departedParticipantIds`, and a restart. The engine's `packages/engine/test/seat-changes.test.ts` covers the command. Web logic is in `apps/web/test/owner-controls.test.ts`. The Online Bot failure and the owner resuming the Bot afterwards is covered by `apps/server/test/online-bot-failures.test.ts`.

## Browser check

On 3 November 2026, using the [schedule browser fixture](../local-development.md#schedule-browser-fixture) (system clock, automatic Bots; API port 3142, control port 6042, Vite port 5242, all stopped afterwards, nothing left running) and one Chrome session. Ann created a 4-seat Online Table with the default 30 s limit and opened Table options.

- The owner sees "Add a Bot" and "Table rules" beside the players; a Guest would not (the controls need `viewerParticipantId === ownerParticipantId`). Ann added "Bot 1" with the form's defaults: it sat in the first open Seat with the default buy-in and the form suggested "Bot 2". She sat down, and Automatic Dealing dealt Hands with the Bot.
- Between Hands the Bot's row offered "Sit out", "Stand up · cash out" and "Remove Bot". During a Hand it read "Sit out after this hand" and "Remove Bot after this hand", with "Stand up" disabled. "Remove Bot after this hand" asked for confirmation ("Bot 1 plays out this hand, then stands up and leaves. Its name stays taken."). Confirming showed "Being removed after this hand" on the row, and the view listed the pending `remove`.
- Adding "Bot 3" during that Hand worked: it took Seat 4 and was outside the Hand (`inHand` absent). When the Hand ended Bot 1 had stood up, cashing out its 2,020 chips, and was listed in `departedParticipantIds`; Table options no longer listed it. Ann's own Timeout Action sat her out the same way (an After-Hand Request), and she resumed with "Return to play".
- In "Table rules", with no Hand in progress, 15 seconds and "Allow seat changes" off applied: the summary line read "15 seconds to act · Live Analysis allowed · Seat changes not allowed", and the view showed `actionTimeLimitSeconds: 15` and `allowSeatChanges: false`. During the next Hand the form was disabled with "Rules change between hands, once the payout has been shown."
- Not seen in the browser (the check used a single Guest): another Guest's screen receiving the changes and the owner not being offered Removal of a Guest who has left. Both are covered by `owner-controls.test.ts` and `removal.test.ts`. The Trainer's snapshot references go stale with every redraw while the clock ticks, so the clicks were made through `eval` of the real buttons.
